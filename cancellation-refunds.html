Billing Policy

Cancellation and refunds.

Last updated August 13, 2026. Billing is not open during early access, and no payment is required.

When billing opens

Dominion will show the price, renewal terms, cancellation steps, and payment processor before anyone checks out.

Paid cancellation terms do not apply during early access because no payment is required.

Refunds

There are no membership charges to refund during early access. Refund terms will be presented before billing opens.

Account data

Billing and account data are separate. Signed-in members can request a data export or account deletion from Profile.

Need help?

Visit Support if an invited account is not recognized. Include only the email on your Dominion account and a short description; never send a password or private journal entry.
